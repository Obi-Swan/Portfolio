About me

Technology has always been a way to make work clearer and more effective.

My career has always been guided by the same purpose: using digital technology to simplify complex processes and help people work more effectively.

I began in education, where I designed digital learning resources and interactive content. Working with the French Embassy in Lithuania strengthened my ability to operate in international and multicultural environments.

As a Training Manager, I had already designed a low-code business application connected to Google Sheets. This gave me an early foundation in workflow digitalisation, structured data and user-focused application design before I moved to Microsoft Power Platform.

My Master's degree in learning engineering developed my approach to user experience, knowledge transfer and digital transformation. Motion design strengthened my visual communication and interface-design skills.

Today, I apply this combination to maritime logistics: I analyse operational processes, design solution architectures, build business applications, automate workflows and create decision-support dashboards.

My strength lies in translating real operational challenges into practical digital solutions that users can understand and adopt.
