Trade Cars Inspection App

A guided field application designed to standardise vehicle inspections, photographs, damage classification, CMR information and signatures.

Power AppsPower AutomateMobile UXDigital Inspection
Project origin

Created from a practical field need.

I designed this application to reduce missing information, inconsistent damage records and incomplete CMR documentation. The solution was validated by Andrew Mortimer, Operations Manager - Port Operations, Calais & Dunkerque.

My responsibility

Complete mobile workflow design.

I designed the inspection journey, mandatory checks, structured damage fields, photograph capture, CMR guidance, signatures and automatic reporting.

Key workflow

Designed around the operator and the vehicle.

  • Step-by-step vehicle inspection
  • Mandatory checks to reduce omissions
  • Front, rear, left and right photographs
  • Structured damaged element, type and location
  • Dirty and wet condition warnings
  • Automatic CMR guidance
  • Digital driver signature
  • Inspection editing and review
  • Standardised evidence for damage reports
