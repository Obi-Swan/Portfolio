Dunkirk-Dover Turnaround Performance

A Power BI operational-excellence platform for port time, sea time, schedule, volumes, dwell time, capacity and team-level analysis.

Power BIPower QueryDAXOperational KPIs
Project origin

Continuous-improvement reporting.

The project was requested by Andrew Mortimer, Operations Manager - Port Operations, Calais & Dunkerque, who defined the operational KPI needs for the Dunkirk-Dover route.

My responsibility

Data, measures and report experience.

I built the Power Query transformations, data model, DAX measures, analytical pages, navigation and visuals according to DFDS templates.

Capabilities

Route-level and individual-sailing analysis.

  • Departure punctuality bands
  • Average port and sea time
  • Loading and discharge duration
  • Average vessel capacity
  • Freight, tourist and passenger volumes
  • Coach and motorcycle volumes
  • Tourist and freight dwell time
  • Layover performance
  • Weekly port-time trends
  • Supervisor and loader comparison
  • Tide-status monitoring
  • Route, vessel, sailing and date filters
