Rosslare Performance & Predictive Simulator

A Power BI platform combining operational reporting, schedule analysis, delay investigation and predictive resource simulation.

Power BIPower QueryDAXScenario Simulation
Project origin

Continuous-improvement requirement.

The report was requested by Andrew Mortimer, Operations Manager - Port Operations, Calais & Dunkerque. He defined the operational questions and KPI needs required for route performance and planning.

My responsibility

Complete analytical implementation.

I translated the requirements into Power Query transformations, data modelling, DAX measures, navigation, DFDS-aligned design and the predictive simulator.

Learning agility

My first Power BI project became a decision-support platform.

I had never used Power BI before this work. I independently learned Power Query, data modelling, DAX and report design while delivering a solution based on real operational requirements.

Capabilities

From historical KPIs to scenario planning.

  • Global and vessel-level KPIs
  • Turnaround-time monitoring
  • Loading and discharge duration
  • Accompanied and unaccompanied volumes
  • Lane-metre and tug analysis
  • STA versus ATA variance
  • STD versus ATD variance
  • Delay reasons and comments
  • Predictive duration simulation
  • Estimated tug requirements
  • Schedule-compliance indicators
