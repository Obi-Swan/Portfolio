Terminal Operating System

A configurable digital operating platform for parking, check-in, vessel activity, dangerous goods, loading, discharge, pets, communication and reporting.

Power AppsSharePointPower AutomatePower BISolution Architecture
Project origin

Requested operational digitalisation.

The project was requested by Andrew Mortimer, Operations Manager - Port Operations, Calais & Dunkerque to standardise and digitalise terminal operations.

My responsibility

Architecture and end-to-end implementation.

I translated the operational objective into the solution architecture, data model, modules, business rules, user experience and technical implementation.

My role

From process analysis to documented architecture.

  • Operational process analysis
  • Application and data architecture
  • SharePoint data-model design
  • Power Apps development
  • Power Automate workflows
  • Parking-allocation business rules
  • Automatic slot-suggestion logic
  • User roles and approval management
  • Operational reporting
  • Functional and technical Visio documentation
  • Testing and continuous improvement
Architecture

A modular and configurable operational system.

Operational lifecycle

Check-in, parking, loading, discharge, pickup and departure states are connected through shared identifiers and timestamps.

Parking intelligence

Dynamic block allocation and multi-factor scoring support consistent slot suggestions.

Configurable rules

Block types, overflow rules, safety restrictions and allocation settings can evolve through configuration.

Before / after

From fragmented coordination to connected operations.

Before

  • Disconnected operational tools
  • Parking decisions based heavily on individual knowledge
  • Repeated manual communication
  • Limited shared traceability
→

After

  • Centralised operational platform
  • Rule-based parking suggestions
  • Automated reports and emails
  • Shared lifecycle and audit information
