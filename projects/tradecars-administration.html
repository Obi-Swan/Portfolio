Case study · Self-initiated application

Trade Cars Administration Platform

A business application created from my own operational experience to manage vehicle reception, stock, documentation, reporting and departure preparation.

Power AppsSharePointPower AutomateOffice ScriptsExcel Online
Project origin

Designed to solve my own operational need.

I identified recurring manual tasks, fragmented information and reporting risks while managing Trade Cars activities. I proposed the digital solution, which was validated by Andrew Mortimer, Operations Manager - Port Operations, Calais & Dunkerque.

My responsibility

Independent design and delivery.

I analysed the workflow, designed the application architecture, structured the data, developed the interface and created the related automations.

My role

End-to-end business application ownership.

  • Vehicle reception and check-in workflow
  • Client and delivery tracking
  • Parking and stock management
  • Departure creation
  • Vehicle embarkation selection
  • Loading-list preparation
  • Photograph and document library
  • Automatic damage-report generation
  • Automatic stock reporting
  • Email distribution
  • Multi-client operational support
Before / after

From repetitive administration to a connected workflow.

Before

  • Information spread across manual sources
  • Repeated stock and reporting actions
  • Higher risk of missing departure information
→

After

  • Centralised vehicle and delivery data
  • Automated stock communication
  • Structured departure and documentation workflow
